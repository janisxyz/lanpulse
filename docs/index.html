Privacy Policy for LanPulse

Effective / last updated: 22 August 2026
App: LanPulse
Package name: com.lanpulse.app
Developer: Janis Schelling (shizoghost)
Contact: shizoghost@exdonuts.com

Short version: LanPulse does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to us or to any third party. Scan results, custom names, theme, language, and optional SSH passwords stay on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no ads, and no cloud backend.

LanPulse is a local-network utility. It finds devices on networks your phone is already attached to (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, VPN, hotspot), shows ping and open ports, and can open an in-app SSH session to a host you choose.

1. Who we are

This policy is issued by Janis Schelling, operating as shizoghost, for the Android application LanPulse. Questions: shizoghost@exdonuts.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect nothing off the device.

  • No Google account or LanPulse account
  • No analytics, advertising, crash, or telemetry SDKs
  • No remote configuration or cloud database
  • Scan results are not uploaded

The INTERNET permission is used only to talk to devices on your attached networks (ICMP, TCP, mDNS, SSDP, SSH). The app does not send those results to us.

3. Data that stays on the device

  • Scan results (IP address, MAC address, hostname, vendor, open ports, ping) live in memory while the app is open.
  • Custom device names you type are stored in app preferences on this phone.
  • SSH username, port, and password (only if you tick “remember”) are stored in app preferences on this phone so you can reconnect. Passwords are never logged and never sent to us.
  • Language and theme stay in app preferences on this phone.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app, so names, SSH credentials, and settings are not copied to Google cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

4. How on-device data is used

Only to provide the features you asked for: listing hosts, showing ping and ports, remembering a label or SSH login you typed, and restoring your theme. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or resale.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — probe hosts on attached LANs and open SSH.
  • Wi-Fi state / nearby Wi-Fi devices — read SSID, band, and link stats for the Discover header. On Android 13+ the nearby-Wi-Fi permission is requested with neverForLocation.
  • Approximate / precise location — required by Android (especially Android 8–12) to read the Wi-Fi SSID. LanPulse does not use GPS, does not track you, does not access location in the background, and does not send location anywhere.
  • Multicast — mDNS / Bonjour hostname discovery on the LAN.

6. Location and Wi-Fi name

Android treats the Wi-Fi network name as location-related. That is the only reason location permission appears. If you deny it, scanning still works; the app may simply omit the SSID in the header.

7. SSH sessions

An SSH session is a direct connection from your phone to a host on your network. Credentials go only to that host. If you choose “remember”, they are stored in private app preferences on the phone, not in cloud backup, and not on our servers. We cannot recover a forgotten password.

8. Children

LanPulse is a network-administration tool. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country) and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

9. Retention and deletion

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. You can delete on-device data at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling LanPulse.

10. Sharing, sale, and third parties

We do not sell personal information. We do not share data with advertisers, analytics vendors, or other processors. The app does not include third-party tracking SDKs. Traffic you start (for example SSH to a Raspberry Pi) goes only to the host you picked.

11. Security

On-device preferences are sandboxed by Android. Backup is off so optional SSH passwords are not copied to cloud backup. SSH uses the SSH protocol to the host you choose. There is no developer backend to encrypt “in transit” to us, because nothing is sent to us.

12. International transfers

We do not transfer personal data internationally because we do not collect it.

13. Your rights (EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws)

You can access, correct, or delete anything the app stored by editing names in the app, forgetting SSH logins, or uninstalling. Because we hold no personal data about you, a data-subject request to us will typically confirm that we have nothing on file. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used under the CCPA/CPRA. To make a request or ask a question, email shizoghost@exdonuts.com.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Continued use after a change means you have seen the new text.
