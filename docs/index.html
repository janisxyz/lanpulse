Privacy Policy for LanPulse

Effective date: 22 August 2026
App: LanPulse
Package: com.lanpulse.app
Developer: Janis Schelling (shizoghost)
Contact: shizoghost@exdonuts.com

LanPulse is a local network utility. It finds devices on networks your phone is already attached to (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, VPN, hotspot), shows ping and open ports, and can open an in-app SSH session to a host you choose.

Data we collect

LanPulse does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to us or to any third-party analytics, ads, or crash service.

  • No accounts
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No cloud backend

The INTERNET permission is used only to talk to devices on your attached networks (ICMP, TCP, mDNS, SSH). The app does not upload scan results.

Data that stays on the device

  • Scan results (IP, MAC, hostname, vendor, open ports, ping) live in memory while the app is open. They are not written to our servers.
  • Custom device names you type are stored in app preferences on this phone.
  • SSH username (and password only if you tick “remember”) are stored in app preferences on this phone so you can reconnect quickly. Passwords are never logged.
  • Language and theme stay in app preferences on this phone.

Android backup is disabled for this app, so names, SSH credentials, and settings are not copied to cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — probe hosts on attached LANs and open SSH.
  • Wi-Fi state / nearby Wi-Fi devices — read SSID, band, and link stats for the Discover header.
  • Location (fine / coarse) — required by Android to read the Wi-Fi SSID on many versions. LanPulse does not use GPS, does not track you, and does not send location anywhere.
  • Multicast — mDNS / Bonjour hostname discovery on the LAN.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
