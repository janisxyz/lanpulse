LanPulse is a local network utility. It finds devices on networks your phone is already attached to, shows ping and open ports, and can open an in-app SSH session.

## Data we collect

LanPulse does **not** collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

- No accounts
- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising
- No cloud backend

`INTERNET` is used only to talk to devices on attached LANs. Scan results are not uploaded.

## Data that stays on the device

Custom names, optional SSH credentials, language, and theme are stored in app preferences on this phone. Android backup is off. Uninstall deletes them.

## Permissions

- Network — LAN probes and SSH
- Wi-Fi / nearby devices — SSID and link stats
- Location — Android requires it to read SSID; the app does not use GPS or send location
- Multicast — mDNS hostnames
